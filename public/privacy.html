Privacy Policy for Vocabify

Last updated: August 24, 2026

What Vocabify Does

Vocabify is a Chrome extension that helps you learn vocabulary by translating words and sentences you select while browsing, and scheduling spaced-repetition reviews.

Data We Collect

  • Saved vocabulary: Words, phrases and sentences you explicitly choose to save, with their translations, example sentences and review progress.
  • Learning statistics: Review scores, streaks and study session data used to produce your learning insights.
  • Device identifier: A randomly generated ID that associates your data with your device. It is not linked to your identity and requires no account.

How Data Is Stored

  • Saved vocabulary and learning statistics are stored on your device and synced to our backend (Convex) so spaced repetition and insights work.
  • Translations are cached on your device (IndexedDB) for up to 30 days to avoid repeat lookups. The cache is capped and pruned automatically.
  • Your OpenAI API key, if you provide one, is stored only on your device (chrome.storage.local). It is never sent to our backend and never leaves your browser except in requests to OpenAI.
  • No account or login is required.

What Is Sent To Third Parties

Vocabify contacts external services only when you actively use a feature. What is sent depends on the feature.

Translating a word or sentence

When you select text and the translation popup opens, we send:

  • the word or sentence you selected;
  • the sentence surrounding it on the page, so the translation matches the intended meaning (for example, "bank" by a river versus a bank that lends money);
  • the page title and the site's domain name (for example example.com), as additional context for the same purpose.

Where that goes depends on your settings:

Explain and Simplify (Reading Assistant)

These optional features send text to OpenAI through our backend, using our API key rather than yours:

  • Explain a word: the word and the sentence it appeared in.
  • Simplify this page: up to 12,000 characters of the visible text of the page you are reading.

Use them only on pages whose content you are comfortable sending to OpenAI.

Pronunciation (text-to-speech)

The word or sentence being pronounced is sent to whichever voice engine you have selected: Google Translate TTS (translate.googleapis.com), OpenAI (api.openai.com, using your own key), or your browser's built-in voices, which send nothing over the network.

YouTube

On YouTube, the extension reads subtitle text already present on the page so it can highlight words you are learning. Nothing is sent anywhere for this.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • No personally identifiable information (name, email, address)
  • No browsing history, and no page content beyond what is described above
  • No health, financial or location data
  • No authentication credentials or passwords
  • We do not read pages you have added to the excluded-sites list, and the extension never acts on text typed into inputs, password fields or editors

Data Sharing

We do not sell your data or share it for advertising. Text is sent to the services listed above solely to return a translation, definition or audio clip, and only when you trigger that feature.

Data Deletion

  • Removing the extension deletes all data stored on your device, including your API key and the translation cache.
  • Data synced to our backend is keyed to your device identifier and is not removed automatically when you uninstall. To have it deleted, email the address below with your device identifier, which is shown in Settings.
